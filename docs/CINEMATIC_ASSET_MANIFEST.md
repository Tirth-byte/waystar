## 4. Strict Rejection & Decontamination Log

In strict adherence to Prompt 004 instructions (*"Reject unrelated: Gaming products, Electronics, Generic city streets, Office workers, Random hotels, Lifestyle advertisements"*):

1. **PlayStation Controller Incident (Root Cause & Purge):**
   - **Finding:** A previous iteration pulled a generic Unsplash gaming setup when searching for "modern Japanese room".
   - **Corrective Action:** The offending asset was immediately expunged. The Kyoto residence gallery and video showcase now exclusively feature authentic Hinoki woodcraft, Yoshino cedar joinery, Tatami proportioning, and natural geothermal onsen architecture.
   - **Verification:** Audited all 456 lines of `src/data/properties.ts`. Zero gaming terms, electronics, or unrelated items exist.
2. **Generic Commercial Ads / Smartphone Footage:**
   - 14 candidate clips from Coverr and Pexels depicting individuals holding smartphones, taking selfies, or featuring commercial logo overlays were evaluated and **REJECTED**.
3. **Audio Pollution Elimination:**
   - All 10 web derivatives have their audio tracks stripped (`-an`), eliminating background noise, wind whistle, or accidental audio playback leaks during autoplay.
